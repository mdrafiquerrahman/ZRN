Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Answers to Your Solar Energy Questions

1. Which solar solution is best for my property?

The ideal solution depends on your energy consumption, property type, available space, and budget. Our experts conduct a detailed assessment before making recommendations.

2. Can residential customers receive government subsidies?

Eligible residential customers may qualify for benefits under PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana, subject to prevailing government guidelines.

3. How much roof space is required for rooftop solar?

The space requirement varies depending on system size. Our team evaluates available roof area during the site survey.

4. Are commercial solar systems profitable?

Yes. Commercial solar systems often provide substantial savings on electricity costs and attractive long-term returns.

5. How long do solar panels last?

Most quality solar panels are designed to operate efficiently for 25 years or more with proper maintenance.

6. Do solar water pumps require fuel?

No. Solar water pumps operate using solar energy, eliminating fuel expenses.

7. Are solar water heaters suitable for businesses?

Yes. Hotels, hospitals, hostels, restaurants, and commercial buildings frequently benefit from solar water heating systems.

8. How much maintenance does a solar system require?

Solar systems generally require minimal maintenance, including occasional cleaning and periodic inspections.

9. Can industrial facilities benefit from solar energy?

Absolutely. Industrial solar systems help reduce energy expenses, improve sustainability, and support long-term operational efficiency.

10. How can I get started?

Contact ZRN Solar to schedule a free consultation and site survey. Our team will recommend the most suitable solar solution for your needs.
