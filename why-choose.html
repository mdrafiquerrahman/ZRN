Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Answers to Your Solar Energy Questions

1. Why should I choose ZRN Solar over other solar companies?

ZRN Solar combines quality installations, customized solutions, professional support, and a customer-first approach to deliver long-term value and reliable performance.

2. Does ZRN Solar provide complete solar installation services?

Yes. We provide end-to-end support including consultation, site survey, system design, equipment procurement, professional installation, net-metering setup, and ongoing support.

3. Are ZRN Solar systems suitable for homes and businesses?

Absolutely. We offer highly customized systems engineered to suit the exact load and structural needs of residential, commercial, industrial, and agricultural clients.

4. How much can I save by installing solar?

Savings vary depending on electricity consumption, system capacity, and local utility tariffs. Many residential and commercial customers cut their utility bills by up to 90%.

5. Does ZRN Solar help with PM Surya Ghar applications?

Yes. We assist eligible residential customers with documentation, portal registration, application status tracking, and net-metering approvals under the government scheme guidelines.

6. How long do solar systems last?

Quality solar systems are designed to provide reliable performance for 25 years or more. ZRN Solar structures and panels are backed by comprehensive performance warranties.

7. Are solar systems safe?

Yes. When installed correctly using quality components, proper grounding, and safety devices (like surge protectors and circuit breakers), solar systems are extremely safe.

8. What maintenance is required?

Solar systems require minimal maintenance, primarily occasional cleaning of panels with water to remove dust/debris, and periodic checkups of electrical cables and connections.

9. Can solar increase property value?

Many property owners view solar installations as a valuable long-term asset due to energy savings, grid independence, and sustainability benefits, which directly increase resale value.

10. How do I get started?

Simply contact ZRN Solar via phone, email, or WhatsApp to schedule a free consultation and site survey. Our experts will assess your roof and recommend the best setup for your requirements.
