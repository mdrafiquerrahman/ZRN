Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Answers to Your Solar Energy Questions

1. How much can I save with a solar system?

Savings depend on electricity consumption, system size, and local electricity tariffs. Many customers experience significant reductions in monthly electricity bills.

2. Is rooftop solar suitable for my property?

Our team conducts a site survey to evaluate roof space, orientation, shading conditions, and energy requirements.

3. How long do solar panels last?

Most high-quality solar panels are designed to perform efficiently for 25 years or more with proper maintenance.

4. Does ZRN Solar provide installation services?

Yes. We provide complete consultation, design, installation, and support services.

5. Can I apply for government subsidies?

Eligible residential consumers may apply under PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana subject to government guidelines.

6. What maintenance does a solar system require?

Solar systems require minimal maintenance, mainly periodic cleaning and routine inspections.

7. How long does installation take?

The installation timeline depends on project size and site conditions. Most residential systems can be completed within a few days after approvals.

8. Do solar systems work during cloudy weather?

Yes. Solar panels continue generating electricity during cloudy conditions, although output may be reduced.

9. Can businesses benefit from solar energy?

Absolutely. Commercial solar systems help reduce operating expenses and improve long-term profitability.

10. Why choose ZRN Solar?

We offer customized solutions, quality installations, professional support, and a customer-focused approach designed to deliver long-term value.
