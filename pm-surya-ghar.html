India's Largest Residential Rooftop Solar Initiative

What is PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana?

PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana is a Government of India initiative designed to promote rooftop solar adoption among residential consumers.

The scheme encourages households to install solar panels on their rooftops and generate clean electricity for their own consumption. By adopting solar energy, homeowners can significantly reduce their electricity bills while contributing to India's renewable energy goals.

The program also provides financial assistance through government subsidies for eligible residential rooftop solar installations, subject to prevailing government guidelines.

Key Objectives of the Scheme

Promote rooftop solar adoption
Reduce household electricity expenses
Increase renewable energy generation
Support environmental sustainability
Encourage energy independence
Accelerate India's clean energy transition

Financial Support for Rooftop Solar Adoption

Understanding Government Subsidies

One of the key advantages of PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana is the availability of government subsidies for eligible rooftop solar systems.

The subsidy is intended to reduce the upfront cost of installation and make solar energy more accessible for homeowners.

Important Information

Government subsidy amounts:

Are determined by official Government of India guidelines.
May vary based on system capacity and applicable rules.
Are subject to periodic updates.
Are available only to eligible applicants.

Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)

Eligible subsidy amounts are generally transferred through the Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) mechanism after successful installation and approval processes as per official guidelines.

Maximizing the Benefits of Your Rooftop Solar System

Understanding Net-Metering

Net-metering is an important component of many rooftop solar systems.

It allows eligible consumers to receive credit for excess electricity generated and exported to the utility grid, subject to applicable regulations and utility policies.

Benefits of Net-Metering

Better utilization of solar energy
Improved savings potential
Efficient energy management
Enhanced return on investment
